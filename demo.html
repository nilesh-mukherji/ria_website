we don't break.

A firm built by

built the category.

Founded in 2016 by the Oracle leaders who shipped the original Revenue Management product. Nine years later: a global firm, a 180-strong specialist bench, and a portfolio of clients most consultancies would acquire us to access.

The story

Nine years.
One obsession.

We left Oracle to build the firm we wished existed — one that paired the product depth of a founding team with the delivery muscle of a tier-one consultancy. The market rewarded that bet.

2016 · FOUNDING

The original ORMB team starts over.

RIA Advisory is founded in Coral Gables, FL by a small group of senior Oracle leaders — the same people who built and grew the Oracle Revenue Management & Billing business from inception.

2018 · GLOBAL

Offices on three continents.

India, Philippines, and the U.K. open within 24 months. Follow-the-sun delivery becomes a structural advantage.

2020 · IP

Accelerators ship.

Proprietary platform accelerators reduce configuration timelines by 40–60% across the standard reference architectures.

2022 · GROWTH

Canaccord Genuity advises on growth equity.

Capital raise to expand vertical coverage in Healthcare and Public Sector — both now flagship practices.

2023 · MERGER

TMG Consulting joins the firm.

The combination creates the most comprehensive suite of revenue-management solutions in the market — utilities depth meets financial services scale.
